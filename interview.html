👨‍💼 Common HR Questions

Click the + button to view a sample approach.

Tell me about yourself.

Give a short introduction covering your education, technical skills, projects, achievements and career interests. Keep the answer relevant to the position.

What are your strengths?

Mention two or three genuine strengths such as problem-solving, teamwork, adaptability or communication. Support them with a short example.

What is your weakness?

Mention a real but manageable weakness and explain what you are doing to improve it. Avoid saying that you have no weaknesses.

Why should we hire you?

Connect your skills, projects, learning ability and enthusiasm with the requirements of the role.

Where do you see yourself in 5 years?

Discuss your professional growth, skills you want to develop and the kind of responsibilities you hope to handle in the future.

Why do you want to join our company?

Research the company beforehand and connect its work, values or products with your interests and career goals.

What is your biggest achievement?

Choose an academic, project or extracurricular achievement and explain what you did, what challenges you faced and what you learned.

How do you handle pressure?

Explain how you prioritize tasks, remain organized and break difficult problems into smaller manageable steps.

💻 Technical Interview Topics

Important technical subjects to revise before your placement interview.

🐍

Programming

C, C++, Java, Python, JavaScript, functions, arrays, strings, OOP and basic problem solving.

🗄️

DBMS

SQL, normalization, keys, transactions, joins, indexing and database concepts.

⚙️

Operating Systems

Processes, threads, scheduling, memory management, paging and deadlocks.

🌐

Computer Networks

OSI model, TCP/IP, HTTP, DNS, IP addresses, routing and network protocols.

🧩

Object Oriented Programming

Classes, objects, inheritance, polymorphism, abstraction, encapsulation and interfaces.

📊

Data Structures

Arrays, linked lists, stacks, queues, trees, searching, sorting and basic complexity.

⭐ Interview Tips

Small things that can make a big difference during your interview.

📚

Know Your Resume

Be prepared to explain every skill, project, internship and achievement mentioned on your resume.

🗣️

Communicate Clearly

Speak clearly and confidently. Take a moment to understand the question before answering.

🔍

Research the Company

Learn about the company's products, services, culture and the role before attending the interview.

⏰

Be On Time

Join online interviews early and reach the interview location with sufficient time.

😊

Stay Confident

If you don't know an answer, be honest and explain how you would approach learning or solving the problem.

📝

Practice Mock Interviews

Practice answering questions aloud so that you become more comfortable speaking during the actual interview.
